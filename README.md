## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and economy balancing:

### 🛩️ Multi-Tier Smuggling Operations (Land, Sea & Air)

* **Coke Plane Smuggling:** Fly a Dodo aircraft across the ocean to Cayo Perico Island, retrieve packages of cocaine bricks from remote drop zones, and navigate back to the hangar safely.
* **Weed Ground Transport:** Pilot high-speed ground vehicles across Los Santos and Blaine County to collect packed weed bricks under tight mission deadlines.
* **Meth Boat Operations:** Navigate coastal and offshore waters on a Dinghy boat to secure pseudoephedrine/meth bricks from water drop points.

### 🏝️ Built-In Cayo Perico Island Support

* **Native IPL & Stream Manager:** Automatically requests and loads all Cayo Perico Island IPLs without requiring extra island mapping resources.
* **Minimap & Radar Fix:** Corrects radar boundaries and interior positioning for the island seamlessly.
* **Ambient Audio & Nodes:** Activates island ambient sound zones, scenario peds, and navigation path nodes when near Cayo Perico.

### ⏱️ Real-Time Mission & Economy Systems

* **Live On-Screen Timer HUD:** Displays a synchronized countdown clock showing remaining minutes and seconds to complete the smuggling run.
* **Deposit & Return Guarantee:** Requires a mission entry deposit that is refunded with an extra return payout once the vehicle is returned in functional condition.
* **Configurable Contraband Yields:** Players receive inventory items (`cokebrick`, `weedbrick`, `methbrick`) with randomized quantity ranges defined per location.

### 🚗 Vehicle Monitoring & State Synchronization

* **Engine Health Monitoring:** Active background thread checks engine health and vehicle existence; if destroyed or totaled, the mission immediately terminates in failure.
* **Server-Wide State Synchronization:** Synchronizes in-progress missions across all clients to prevent players from taking the same contract at the same time.
* **Automated Reset Timers:** Server-side threads automatically clean up and reset mission availability if a player disconnects or abandons the mission.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence

* **Multi-Framework & Target Support:** Seamlessly works with ESX, QB, QBX, and various target systems (ox_target, qb-target, qtarget).
* **Highly Secure:** Built-in server-side exploit checks, including distance validation and trigger authentication, to prevent cheating.
* **Marked Bills & Vehicle Integrations:** Supports marked bills/black money with item metadata worth handling, alongside automated fuel (100%) and vehicle keys handling via `ak47_lib`.
